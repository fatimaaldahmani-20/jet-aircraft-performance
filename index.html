Assumptions

Jet aircraft, constant thrust at the selected altitude, steady-level unaccelerated flight, dry ideal air, and constant aerodynamic coefficients.

Inputs outside the supported ranges are rejected. Speeds below stall or above Mach 0.7 are excluded from valid results. Missing intersections and no-climb cases are reported explicitly.

Runs entirely in the browser. No backend or external calculation service is required.
